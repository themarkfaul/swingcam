SwingCam · phone check (M0)

Run this on the camera phone in Safari. It measures what this iPhone can do, then gives you a report to send back.

1 · Start

Tap Start and allow camera and microphone.

– fps

2 · Automatic checks

Keep the phone still, pointed at something. Takes about a minute. The preview will switch lenses on its own.

Test clip (last 3.5 s of the encoder test)

Did it play, and look smooth?

3 · Microphone level

– dBFS now · peak – dBFS

4 · Clap test (audio lag)

Stand about 2 m in front of the camera with your hands in view. Tap the button, wait for CLAP, then clap once, sharply. Then tap the first picture where your hands touch. Do it 3 times.

5 · Screen-awake test

First set Settings → Display & Brightness → Auto-Lock to 30 seconds. Then tap Start and don't touch the phone for 2 minutes.

Afterwards you can set Auto-Lock back.

6 · Record a range sample

At the range: set the phone where it will sit for real and record a few minutes of your swings, including other golfers nearby. These become test files for the swing detector. Up to 10 minutes per recording.

7 · Send the report

Share it to yourself (Mail or Notes), then paste it to Claude.
